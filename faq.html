Ordering & Delivery

Ordering & Delivery

Once you check out, log in to your account and open your dashboard — you'll see a live status rail moving through Received, Preparing, In the Oven, Out for Delivery and Delivered, updated in real time.
You can request changes or a cancellation by calling us at (503) 555-0192 within a few minutes of ordering. Once your pizza enters the oven, we usually can't stop the bake, but we'll always try to accommodate you.
Most orders arrive within 20–30 minutes of placing them, and you can watch every stage live from your dashboard once you're logged in.
Contact us right away through the Contact page or by phone — we'll re-fire a fresh pizza or issue a refund, no questions asked. We stand behind every order that leaves our oven.
We currently deliver within a 5-mile radius of 214 Mill Street. Enter your address at checkout and we'll let you know instantly if you're in range.
Yes — at checkout choose "Schedule for later" and pick a time up to 5 days ahead. We'll start baking right on time so it arrives hot.
Menu & Dietary

Menu & Dietary

Yes — every pizza can be made on a gluten-friendly crust or with dairy-free cheese for a small upcharge. Note that our kitchen isn't fully gluten-free, so trace cross-contact is possible.
Every build starts from a base price for your chosen size and crust, then each topping adds a flat fee shown live as you tap — the running total updates instantly so there are never surprises at checkout.
Most of our produce comes from the Riverside Farmers Market, just a few miles from our oven. Our San Marzano tomatoes are imported, since true San Marzanos only grow near Naples.
Yes, every item on the Menu page lists common allergens. If you have a severe allergy, please also add a note at checkout so our kitchen can take extra care.
Every pizza on our menu comes in Small (10"), Medium (12") and Large (16"), so you can choose the size that fits your table.
Your Account & Tracking

Your Account & Tracking

No, guest checkout is available. Creating a free account is optional but lets you track orders live, save addresses and reorder favorites in one tap.
Once logged in, add or edit addresses from your dashboard's Addresses tab. Your default address is filled in automatically the next time you check out.
Open your order history in the dashboard, find a past order, and tap Reorder — the exact same pizzas, sizes and toppings are added straight to your cart.
On the Login page, select "Forgot password" and follow the prompts. If you still need help, our team can verify your account manually — just contact us.
Received means we've got your order, Preparing is prep and topping, In the Oven is the 90-second wood-fired bake, Out for Delivery means a rider has it, and Delivered confirms it's at your door.
Payments & Policies

Payments & Policies

Yes — we never store full card numbers on our servers. Payments are processed through an encrypted, PCI-compliant checkout, the same standard used by major card networks.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Apple Pay online, plus cash or card on delivery if you'd prefer to pay at the door.
Yes — browse current offers on our Deals page, then enter the matching code at checkout to apply the discount before you pay.
If we get your order wrong or it doesn't meet our standards, we'll refund it in full or send a fresh pizza — whichever you prefer. Just reach out within 24 hours of delivery.
Tips are never automatically included. You'll see an optional tip step at checkout, and 100% of it goes straight to your delivery rider.
